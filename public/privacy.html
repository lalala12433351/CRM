Pixbe CRM Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Pixbe CRM ("we", "us") is a customer relationship management service for businesses. This policy explains what data the Pixbe CRM web app and the Pixbe CRM mobile apps for Android and iOS collect, why, and how it is handled. The app is used by employees of businesses ("workspaces") that have a Pixbe CRM account. The workspace is the owner of the business data it stores in Pixbe CRM.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your name, work email, phone number, and role, used to sign you in and show your activity to your workspace.
  • CRM data: the leads, notes, tasks, messages, and other records your workspace adds.
  • Call details (Android and iOS): when you call a lead from the app, we record the number dialed, the call start and end time, the talk time, and whether it was answered. On Android this is read from the phone's call history for calls placed from the app. On iOS only the start and end of the call are observed.
  • Call recordings (Android only, optional): if your workspace turns on call recording and you allow access to audio files, the app looks for the recording that your phone's own call recorder saved for a call placed from the CRM, and uploads that single file to your workspace. The app does not record calls itself and does not read other audio files.
  • Contacts (Android, optional): used only on the device to match a recording file named after a contact to the right call. Contacts are not uploaded.
  • Device data: phone manufacturer and Android version, used to show the right setup instructions.

We do not collect your location, and we do not use your data for advertising.

How data is used and shared

  • To provide the CRM to your workspace: logging calls against leads, reports, and playback of recordings by authorised members of your workspace.
  • Data is stored on Amazon Web Services in encrypted storage. Recordings are only accessible through short-lived links issued to signed-in members of the same workspace.
  • We share data only with service providers needed to run the service (hosting, email, and sign-in), or when required by law. We do not sell personal data.

Your choices

  • Every phone permission can be refused or turned off in your phone settings. The app keeps working without it, but calls or recordings will not be logged.
  • Workspace admins can turn call recording off for the whole workspace.
  • You can delete your account from Settings > Profile > Delete account in the app or on the web. This deletes your login and removes you from the workspace. If you are the only admin, the workspace data is deleted within 30 days.

Retention

Workspace data, including call logs and recordings, is kept while the workspace account is active, or until the workspace deletes it.

Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact your workspace admin or email privacy@pixbe.in.
